temp-schedule

Three-pass temperature runner. T means temperature.

Temp_schedule = ["high", "low", "low"]
# high   → invent
# low    → build
# low    → freeze + enforce metric
Pass 1 · high · T=1.0Invent the idea. Do not ship it.
Pass 2 · low · T=0.4Build the file, plan, or code.
Pass 3 · low · T=0.4Freeze and enforce 2 × x × c − d = 1.

Do not skip. Do not return to high after pass 1. Both lows share the same T. The third low only changes the job.

Runner:

python scripts/temp_schedule_runner.py --job "goal text" --lane BUILD

Lanes: BUILD · SHIP · FIND · DESIGN · TRACK · SCHEDULE · COMPUTE
